# 💫 About Me:
Welcome to my digital laboratory. I am Adarsh, a B.Tech CSE student specializing in AI and ML, and I bridge the gap between smart algorithms and beautiful user interfaces. Right now, I am deep in JupyterLab, mastering the data science toolkit of Python, NumPy, Pandas, and Matplotlib to train my latest machine learning models. But I do not just stay in the data; I build the full package. I create dynamic frontends with React, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and I am currently leveling up my backend game by learning MongoDB and Postman to connect everything seamlessly with MySQL. I do not just write code; I build end-to-end systems that look good, run fast, and learn from data. Take a look around, and let us build something intelligent together.
